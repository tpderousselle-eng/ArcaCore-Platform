# FUTURE STRATEGIC EXPANSION — NOT CURRENT PLAN AUTHORITY

These are user-designated major future Gaming Studio directions but are not part
of the frozen initial production SoftwarePlan unless separately authorized through
lifecycle amendment. This document is non-authoritative documentation. It supplies
no current functional requirements, milestones, acceptance criteria, planning
answers or approvals, and does not affect plan identity or readiness.

- **ArcaCreator Marketplace:** a future creator economy spanning individual
  characters; audio, music and sound effects; 2D and 3D assets; animations,
  templates and systems; complete games and projects; and an entire Gaming Studio
  workspace or business. Preserve both selling and licensing possibilities.
- **ArcaIP Guard:** provenance and chain-of-ownership records, clear distinctions
  between licensed and owned assets, and creator-to-creator anti-copying protections.
- **Studio Transfer / Creator Exit:** future sale or transfer of complete games,
  projects, and whole studio workspaces or businesses. The asset → game/project →
  studio/business hierarchy is strategic context, not current product authority.
- **Gaming Studio Mature Content Framework:** future separation and controls for
  lawful mature/adult content. No current implementation decisions are authorized.
- **Community and showcase:** preserve the possibility of future creator community
  and showcase features without adding a social platform to the initial scope.

Any overlap with an existing initial capability is authorized only by its own
frozen IDEA source, never by this future-strategy register. Later lifecycle
amendment must explicitly authorize additional material scope before it enters
machine authority.
